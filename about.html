Engineering Philosophy

I believe reliable software is the result of thoughtful engineering rather than accidental success.

Good systems should be:

  • Simple to understand.
  • Reliable under changing conditions.
  • Secure by design.
  • Easy to maintain.
  • Built with long-term evolution in mind.

Technology changes rapidly, but sound engineering principles remain valuable across every generation of software.
