Projects are the primary evidence of engineering capability. Projects are not presented as finished products. Instead, they represent engineering journeys that evolve over time.

Every project should demonstrate problem solving, technical decision making, system architecture, continuous improvement, documentation, and engineering discipline. Visitors should understand not only what was built, but why it was built and how it continues to evolve.
